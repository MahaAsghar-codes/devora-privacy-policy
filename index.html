Devora Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Devora (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) helps users track coding progress, organize development work, and prepare professional posts.

Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Devora may process coding logs, project details, drafts, app preferences, and information needed to connect your LinkedIn account. Please review the app's actual behavior for the specific data collected and where it is stored.

LinkedIn Connection and Sharing

If you connect LinkedIn, the app may use authorized LinkedIn account information and permissions to support account connection and publishing actions you request. LinkedIn handles its services under its own privacy policy. Review each post before publishing.

AI Features

If you use AI-assisted features, text you submit may be sent to the AI provider configured in the app to generate a response. Avoid submitting passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive information.

Storage and Security

Data storage and security depend on the features and services enabled in your app version. We aim to use appropriate safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Data Sharing and Retention

Data may be processed by third-party services only as needed for features you choose to use. Retention and deletion depend on where the data is stored and the relevant service settings. Confirm these details against the app's implementation before relying on this policy.

Your Choices

You can stop using optional integrations and revoke LinkedIn access through LinkedIn's account settings. You may also remove locally stored app data by using the app's available controls or uninstalling it, where applicable. This may not delete data already sent to third-party services.

Children's Privacy

Devora is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Devora app developer through the contact information provided on the app's official profile or distribution page.

This policy should be reviewed and updated to accurately describe the released app before publication.
